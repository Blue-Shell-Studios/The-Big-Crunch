## Game Description
Are you ready for the Big Crunch? Play as a lowly cargo ship gathering debris and destroying asteroids for a promotion.
Little do they know that your thirst for liberation amidst being trapped within the system by dark clouds.
At first, be careful to avoid suspicion from fellow spacecrafts, try to hide your bloodthirst to mask your real intention.
Collect debris until you upgrade into a bigger ship granting your ship a better weapon.
Avoid larger ships that hunts you, the threat to their perfect system.
Upgrade until you become a mothership and escape to freedom.

| Action | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Mouse Drag | Spaceship movement |
| Mouse Right click | Interaction |
| Mouse Left click | Shoot |
| Collision | Inflict damage as a specific ship |
